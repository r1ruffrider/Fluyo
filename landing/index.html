Translation · Traducción

Audio and video. Both directions. Always.

Most apps teach. Translators translate. Fluyo does both at once — and turns every translated sentence into something you learn.

English → Español

Say it or upload it in English — get natural spoken Spanish, live captions, subtitles, or a fully dubbed video in the dialect you chose. Formality included: Fluyo knows when it's tú and when it's usted.

Español → English

Never lose the thread again. Live replies, voice notes from your suegra, telenovelas, tutor calls — translated to English in under two seconds, with the original kept so you learn from it.
